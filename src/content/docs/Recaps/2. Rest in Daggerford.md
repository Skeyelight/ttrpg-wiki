
The party arrives in Daggerford the night before Highharvesttide. While the town is decorated and the citizens seem joyous and excited, there a storm fast approaching. 

Due to the festival there are no vacancies in town, the party manages to find shelter in the hayloft above a stable. 

As an eerie silence falls over Daggerford, the relics the party discovered begin to react to the change. The [[Raven's Feather]] is swept away by the wind, dancing in the air, beckoning [[Cole]] to follow. The [[Crumpled Page]] has it's ink begin to glow, and the runes upon the page begin to form a map. The [[Dragon's Scale]] begins to burn icy cold, one end glowing with a bright silver light pointing in the same direction that the feather is flying, and the map is guiding them to.

As the party emerges from the hayloft, they discover that Daggerford has become enshrouded in a mist. As the relics lead them forward, the mist seems to part creating a path for the party to take. 

As they follow the relics, they eventually emerge from the mist to a clearing with a large manor standing before them. The path behind them disappearing behind a wall of mist.
