---
title: "Raven's Feather"
---

A small, black feather with soft, downy barbs. When held aloft before a storm, the feather seems to gently tremble, exuding the scent of cold rain and damp earth.The one who possesses the raven's feather should have strong, vivid reasons to believe that the feather will always guide them to those who need aid, protection, and comfort.
